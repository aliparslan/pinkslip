# Where everything lives: a step back (2026-10-13)

The owner asked to reassess the overall layout before going further on
Tracker. Tracker has to hold:
- applications and outreach;
- every stage, from saved through applied, assessment and interviewing;
- the jobs auto-apply is working through.

That's too much for one screen. This doc lists what the app has to show,
then the ways to arrange it.

## What has to live somewhere

| Thing | What it is | How often you look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New jobs | The feed | Daily |
| Search | Jobs and companies | Often |
| Saved | Jobs you might apply to (your shortlist) | Often; grows fastest |
| Applying now | Auto-apply's queue: queued, applying, done | While a batch runs |
| Needs you | Answers to finish an application, interview times, follow-ups due, deadlines | Daily, time-sensitive |
| Applications | Applied → assessment → screen → interview → offer, then closed | Weekly |
| Outreach | People you've emailed: due, waiting, replied | Weekly |
| You | Profile, plan, settings | Rarely |

Stages:
- **Saved.**
- **Applying:** auto-apply only.
- **Applied, then Assessment:** Assessment is new, for online assessments
  and take-homes. The applications table doesn't have it yet.
- **Screen, Interview, Offer.**
- **Closed:** rejected, withdrawn or no reply.

## The options

### A · Today takes the urgent things

Tabs: Today, Jobs, Tracker, You. The Search tab folds into the field on
Jobs.

- **Today:** Needs you (everything time-sensitive), "Applying now", and a
  summary of yesterday. This is already drawn as "28 · Today tab".
- **Tracker:** the record. Applications (stages) and Outreach, with Saved
  as a filter or its own segment.
- **For:** one place to check each morning, and Tracker gets calm.
- **Against:**
  - Today is thin until auto-apply and outreach exist.
  - It needs a fifth tab, or Search loses its tab.

### B · Tracker by phase: To apply · Applied · Outreach

No new tab. Tracker's switch groups by where a job is, not by object.

- **To apply:** "Applying now" (the queue, with progress), anything that
  needs you to finish one, then Saved sorted by deadline.
- **Applied:** Needs you (interview times and so on), then Offer,
  Interview, Screen, Assessment, Applied, and Closed folded at the bottom.
- **Outreach:** follow-up due, waiting, replied.
- **For:**
  - Auto-apply's queue sits with the jobs it's working through (saved
    becomes applying becomes applied).
  - Every segment has two or three sections, so it fits.
  - It survives the move to Today later: Today can mirror the Needs you
    rows and the batch card without Tracker changing shape.
- **Against:** Needs you is split between two segments, and a badge per
  segment has to say where to look.

### C · Tracker today, plus Today later (what's drawn now)

One list (Needs you, batch card, stages, Saved, Closed) with Applications
and Outreach. Saved is reached with a filter (layout options 1–5 on Paper
"Tracker layouts · Oct 13").
- **For:** the smallest change.
- **Against:** it's long, and the batch card plus Needs you push the
  stages below the fold.

### D · A People tab for outreach

Tabs: Jobs, Tracker, People, You. Tracker holds applications only, and
saved jobs live on Jobs.
- **For:** each tab is one kind of thing.
- **Against:**
  - A whole tab for a feature most students use lightly.
  - It splits a company's people from its applications.

## Recommendation

B now, then A's Today when auto-apply ships:
- **Now:** B makes everything fit without a new tab. It also answers "where
  is auto-apply?": in To apply, between the jobs you saved and the ones it
  applied to.
- **When auto-apply ships:** Today opens on Needs you, the batch card and a
  daily summary. Tracker keeps the full detail.
- **The tab bar then:** Search folds into the field on Jobs, so it stays at
  four tabs.
