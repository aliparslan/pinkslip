  owner's "unable to see the text entry field").

    brings up "From your answers" above the keyboard. iOS had already lifted
    the field to just above the keyboard, so the strip covered the field and
    iOS's form bar covered the rest. Reproduced in the simulator on the sample
    form's "Why Stripe?" box.
  - Now the page scrolls the field up above the strip. It adds room at the
    page's end so even the last field can rise, and removes that room when
    the keyboard closes, so the page doesn't jump while you type.
  - Checked in the simulator: the label and the whole box show above the
    strip, including while typing.
  - Earlier today I'd guessed the You pages instead; that fix (the Contact
    fields) is real too and checked below.
  - Needs an over-the-air update.

- **Question cards slid the wrong way** (owner, while answering GitLab's
  questions: "the cards dont go to the right or the left, its kinda random").
  - A sheet's steps slid by their own "inner" flag, which suits a menu and one
    level under it. From question 2 on, Next sent the old card right while the
    new one also came in from the right, and Back brought the earlier card in
    from the right.

    waiting-send questions slide left on Next and right on Back.

## Found, not fixed

1. **Old matches still list foreign jobs until they rebuild.**
   - The location fix only applies to matches rebuilt under the new matcher
     version: 515 of about 135,000 so far.
   - Old matches still include Toronto (14 people) and Bengaluru (4).
   - Alerts only use current matches, so new alerts are clean. Whether the
     feed shows old matches until each person's rebuild wasn't checked.
2. **Row ⋯ menu at phone width (website).**
   - In the browser's phone emulation, the ⋯ on a job row didn't open; at
     desktop width it opens.
   - Could be the touch emulation, not a bug. Check on a real phone (10.6).
3. **A required dropdown isn't outlined.**
   - The sample form's required "sponsorship" dropdown counts in "2 need
     you", but only the text box gets the pink outline.
4. **The guest feed's "Early-career" headline over Senior roles.**
   - The default guest search includes Mid-career (3–5 years), so roles
     titled "Sr." or "Senior" that ask 3–5 years show up.
   - A wording or product call, not a bug.
5. **A job card leads with a non-US place.**
   - Chainguard's "Canada - Remote +3" also lists United States - Remote; the
     card could show the US place first.
6. **"Which application is this?" counts, last 14 days:** Yext 4, Amazon 4,
   Mercury 2, Glean 2.
   - The rule deployed today stops receipts from asking when nothing would
     change; watch whether these drop.
7. **The Kit's own sample doesn't follow the job page's ask rule.**
   - Pressing "I applied" in the sample browser would still ask "Did you
     apply?" after. Kit only; the job page has the fix.
