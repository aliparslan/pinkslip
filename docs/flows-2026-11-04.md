# Every flow (Nov 4, 2026)

Every path through Pinkslip on the iPhone and the website, start to finish,
for the owner to run and send feedback on. It replaces nothing: the
[Nov 4 test plan](test-plan-2026-11-04.md) stays as the short list.

Part 1 is people: nine personas, each a journey through the flows in the
order a real person would hit them. Part 2 is every flow by area, with each
permutation as its own flow. A persona step names flow IDs (C4, H12), so a
problem found on a journey can be filed against one flow.

How to run it:
- Each flow is what to do, then **→** what should happen. Mark it passed,
  or write what happened instead. The checklist page
  (https://claude.ai/artifact/Ffc6NNGBp2X1Ek5rwgFBTs) has the same flows
  with a mark and a note on each, saved where Claude can read them.
- iPhone: your phone on the owner channel, or a spare account where a flow
  says so. Website: Safari on a laptop, then a phone.
- Never press Submit on a real employer's form unless you mean to apply.
  The Kit's sample form (U9) is safe. Sends (I, J) are real; use a job you
  want.
- Flows that delete or sign out (B13–B16) want a spare account.

## Who sees what

What production has on, Nov 4. A flow for a group you're not in can only
be checked from an account that is.

# Part 2: Every flow by area

## A. First launch and onboarding (iPhone)

- [ ] **A1 Fresh install opens onboarding.** Delete the app, install it,
  open it. → The hook: "Job hunting shouldn't be another job." over the
  collage, with Leave it to Pinkslip and I have an account.
- [ ] **A2 Leave it to Pinkslip.** Tap it. → The mess clears and the
  button grows into page two.
- [ ] **A3 What Pinkslip handles.** Read page two, tap I'm ready. → The
  questions sheet over a live feed.
- [ ] **A4 I have an account (from the hook).** On a fresh install, tap I
  have an account. → Onboarding ends and the sign-in sheet opens (B1–B5).
  Dismiss it: → Jobs, with no questions asked.
- [ ] **A5 Career stage.** "Where are you in your career?" Pick one stage,
  then two. → Chips toggle; Continue goes on.
- [ ] **A6 Roles.** "What do you want to do?" Pick Any role; then pick a
  role. → Any role and a specific role don't both stay picked.
- [ ] **A7 Where.** "Where do you want to work?" Remote; Anywhere in the
  US; one or two metros. → Anywhere in the US and metros don't fight; Remote
  can sit with either.
- [ ] **A8 The feed behind.** Each tap changes the jobs and count behind
  the sheet at once.
- [ ] **A9 Show my jobs.** On the last question. → Jobs with that search;
  Your search shows the answers.
- [ ] **A10 Back keeps answers.** Go back a question and forward. → Picks
  are kept.
- [ ] **A11 Nothing picked.** Try to Continue with nothing picked on each
  question. → Note what it does: blocked, or a sensible default.
- [ ] **A12 Kill mid-onboarding.** Force-quit on question 2, reopen. →
  Onboarding again (it isn't finished), not a half-set search.
- [ ] **A13 Second launch.** After onboarding, quit and reopen. → Opens on
  Today, not onboarding.
- [ ] **A14 Small phone.** On an SE-size screen: the collage doesn't crowd
  the headline; buttons aren't cut.

## B. Sign-in, account, sign-out (iPhone)

Where the sign-in sheet opens from: You's card (guest), the hook's I have
an account, You → Account as a guest, a guest's first save (B10), a
company's People "Sign in" (F5). Try B1–B5 from at least two of them.

- [ ] **B1 Continue with Apple, new account.** → Apple's sheet, then
  signed in where you were; your name on You.
- [ ] **B2 Continue with Google.** → Google's page, back in the app,
  signed in.
- [ ] **B3 Continue with email, new.** Your email → Send me a code. →
  "Check your email"; the code arrives from login@pinkslip.work.
- [ ] **B4 Enter the code.** → Signed in. Wrong code: "wrong" message, stays
  on the step. Old code: says it expired, Resend is the main button.
- [ ] **B5 Use my password.** Email → Use my password → Sign in. → Signed
  in. Wrong password: a plain error, nothing else changes.
- [ ] **B6 Make a password after a code.** After B3–B4 on an account with
  no password. → "Save password" step; Save it, or Not now.
- [ ] **B7 Forgot it?** On the password step: Forgot it? Email me a code →
  code → a new password.
- [ ] **B8 Email link.** Tap the link in the sign-in email on the phone. →
  The app opens and signs in ("Signing in"). An old or used link: "This
  sign-in link didn't work" with Back to You.
- [ ] **B9 Alerts ask after sign-in.** First sign-in with alerts not on. →
  "Be first to apply" with Turn on alerts. Turn on: iOS asks once. Not now:
  sheet closes.
- [ ] **B10 Keep your saved jobs.** As a guest, save a job for the first
  time. → The save happens, then "Keep your saved jobs · So they follow you
  to a new phone" with the ways in and Not now. Save again: doesn't ask
  twice.
- [ ] **B11 Guest saves carry over.** Save two jobs as a guest, sign in. →
  Both are in Tracker's Saved, and on the website for that account.
- [ ] **B12 Account page.** You → your card → Account: edit Name, Save;
  Cancel leaves it.
- [ ] **B13 Log out.** Account → Log out. → Guest again; Tracker empty;
  Jobs still works.
- [ ] **B14 Sign back in.** → Saves, Tracker, resume, answers, settings all
  back.
- [ ] **B15 Delete account (spare account).** Account → Delete account →
  confirm. → Signed out; signing in with that method starts empty.
- [ ] **B16 Apple's token revoked.** After B15 on an Apple account: iOS
  Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple. →
  Pinkslip is gone.
- [ ] **B17 Offline sign-in.** Airplane mode, try each way in. → A plain
  offline message, no spinner forever.
- [ ] **B18 Cancel Apple or Google.** Start, then cancel the system sheet.
  → Back on the sign-in sheet, no error toast.
- [ ] **B19 Switch accounts.** Log out of one, sign in to another. → Nothing
  from the first account shows (Tracker, resume, answers, Today).
