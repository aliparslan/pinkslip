# Every app flow, in run order (Nov 4, 2026)

Every path through the Pinkslip iPhone app, as one route to follow from a
fresh install to a deleted spare account. Each stage leaves the app the way
the next one needs it, so go top to bottom. A step's number is its stage and
place (4.3 is stage 4, step 3); the website isn't covered.

Most of it fits in two or three sittings (stages 1–16). Stage 17 is what can
only happen with time: alerts at their hour, asks, company emails. Mark those
over the next day or two.

How to run it:
- Each step is what to do, then **→** what should happen. Mark it passed, or
  write what happened instead. The checklist page
  (https://claude.ai/artifact/Ffc6NNGBp2X1Ek5rwgFBTs) has the same steps
  with a mark and a note on each, and a button that takes you to the first
  one you haven't marked.
- Use your phone on the owner channel. Stage 16 uses a spare account.
- Never press Submit on a real employer's form unless you mean to apply.
  The Kit's sample form (6.2) is safe. Sends in stage 7 are real.

## 1 · Fresh install and onboarding

Delete Pinkslip and install it from TestFlight. Your account and everything
in it stay on the server.

- [ ] **1.1 Fresh install opens onboarding.** Delete the app, install it,
  open it. → The hook: "Job hunting shouldn't be another job." over the
  collage, with Leave it to Pinkslip and I have an account.
- [ ] **1.2 Leave it to Pinkslip.** Tap it. → The mess clears and the button
  grows into page two.
- [ ] **1.3 What Pinkslip handles.** Read page two, tap I'm ready. → The
  questions sheet over a live feed.
- [ ] **1.4 Career stage.** "Where are you in your career?" Pick one stage,
  then two. → Chips toggle; Continue goes on.
- [ ] **1.5 The feed behind.** Each tap changes the jobs and count behind
  the sheet at once.
- [ ] **1.6 Nothing picked.** Try to Continue with nothing picked on each
  question. → Note what it does: blocked, or a sensible default.
- [ ] **1.7 Roles.** "What do you want to do?" Pick Any role; then pick a
  role. → Any role and a specific role don't both stay picked.
- [ ] **1.8 Kill mid-onboarding.** Force-quit on question 2, reopen. →
  Onboarding again (it isn't finished), not a half-set search.
- [ ] **1.9 Where.** "Where do you want to work?" Remote; Anywhere in the
  US; one or two metros. → Anywhere in the US and metros don't fight; Remote
  can sit with either.
- [ ] **1.10 Back keeps answers.** Go back a question and forward. → Picks
  are kept.
- [ ] **1.11 Show my jobs.** On the last question. → Jobs with that search;
  Your search shows the answers.
