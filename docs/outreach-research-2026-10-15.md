# Outreach: research (2026-10-15)

Before drawing Outreach, read on what gets a recruiter to reply and how
mail apps handle a follow-up. Every number below comes from a vendor that
sells outreach or email tools; treat them as direction, not proof.

## The moment

- **Who:** someone who applied, or is about to, and wants a person to see
  it.
- **When:** right after applying, or browsing a company they like.
- **What they need first:** the right person, and words they'd be happy to
  send.
- **Then:** send it, and stop worrying about when to nudge.

## What the reading says

**Follow-ups bring a large share of the replies.**
- Noon (a survey of 1,000 recruiters, 2026): 17.6% replied to cold email;
  most replies came after a follow-up, and the second message alone drew
  27% of them. Its two pages disagree on how much the first email gets.
- Belkins (16.5 million sales emails): replies fall with each follow-up,
  to about 3% by the fourth.
- Timing, where Prospeo and Whali (citing Woodpecker and Instantly)
  overlap: the first follow-up 3–5 business days later, then about a
  week apart, stopping after two or three. That matches what Pinkslip
  already decided on Oct 9: 3 business days, then about a week, at most
  two.

**Short and specific beats long and generic.**
- 50–125 words is the most cited range (Boomerang, older); newer analyses
  point shorter still.
- Personal details tied to a real reason lift replies a lot; a name and a
  company alone barely do (Martal, Infraforge).
- Career-office advice (Yale SOM, Assumption, The Muse): a named person,
  not careers@; say which role, never "any position"; one small ask (a
  short call, which roles fit, a referral); attach the resume; under about
  150 words.

## What apps do (Mobbin)

- [Apple Mail's composer](https://mobbin.com/screens/6e53b4da-62b4-42e6-8bef-53549807ef59):
  a sheet with a round close and a round send at the top, the subject as a
  large title, To and Subject as quiet rows, then the body.
- [Notion Mail](https://mobbin.com/screens/74bffc00-22e1-470e-b9b4-334dc5d33b4c):
  a pill Send in the top right; From shown small above To.
- [Notion Mail's thread](https://mobbin.com/screens/ed80d5fe-1dd4-48aa-977b-8edc5b87629d):
  "Remind on Aug 10 at 8:00 AM" as one strip at the top.
- [Quo](https://mobbin.com/screens/c49dccfa-fdf3-41e2-b1ac-63d30f848e0c):
  "Your scheduled message will be sent Jun 7, 12:00 PM" as a quiet line
  above the composer.

## For us

1. **The person comes first,** with a reason to trust the address (a
   verified mark and where it came from).
2. **Drafts are short:** about 100 words, the role named, one ask, the
   resume attached. The words Pinkslip filled in are highlighted so you can
   check them.
3. **The follow-ups are planned when you send,** and shown as one quiet
   line with dates ("Follows up Oct 20 and Oct 27 if no reply"), not a
   settings form. Two at most.
4. **A reply ends it.** Follow-ups stop themselves when email tracking
   sees a reply.
5. **A thread reads like the application's stub:** what was sent and when,
   what comes next, nothing else.

## Decided (owner, same day)

The round is approved; the screens and rules are in
`redesign-wireframes.md` ("Outreach"). In short: People and a person's
page, no "verified" anywhere, writing through the iPhone's mail sheet with
"Did you send it?" on return, follow-ups planned at send, conversations on
the person's page (not a Tracker section), sending yourself is free, and
Pinkslip's own composer waits for Pro's "Send them for me".
