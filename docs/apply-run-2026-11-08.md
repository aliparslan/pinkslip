## Answers

- **Lever.** Apply for me sends Greenhouse and Ashby forms only, so Lever jobs
  keep plain Apply. Lever's forms put a captcha on Submit, so a Lever send
  would mostly end in "Finish it yourself". It needs its own form reader, as
  Ashby did.
- **The Render job.** Everything I found in it is in the table above: Check
  first was fine (15 of 15), the real send hit a captcha on Submit, then the
  resume chip read as "1 needs you" and the bar stayed after you applied
  yourself. Ashby shows a captcha when it doubts a browser, so Render may keep
  doing that. If something else felt wrong there, tell me where.

## Your call

- **Apply myself, faster still.** The sheet takes 500ms to close before the
  browser can open (iOS opens one at a time). A quicker close for this
  hand-off would be a new motion value.
- **Stop from the bar's queue.** Stop is on the job page; the queue sheet
  could have it per row too.
- **Lever.** Build it, knowing its captcha?
