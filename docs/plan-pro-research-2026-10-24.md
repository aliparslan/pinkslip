# Plan and Pro: research (2026-10-24)

The owner on the first Plan round (Paper "Plan and Pro · Oct 24"): there are
alignment issues, it looks cluttered, the logo on the Pro graphic (the two
slips) is old and too basic, and the Pro graphic isn't designed well. "We
need to do research. Besides just Mobbin this time, try Dribbble too."

## What was wrong with the first round

- **Alignment:** three left edges on one screen. The meter and the Pro card
  set their text 16 in from the card, at 32, while the footnote between them
  sits on the page's 16. In P4 the footnote under the count sits at 16 and
  the one under the group at 32. In the sheet, the terms and Restore are
  centred under left-aligned choices.
- **Clutter:** every fact got its own surface (a meter card, a footnote, the
  Pro card, a group, another footnote). The same promise appears on the
  card and again at the top of the sheet. The legal lines stack two
  footnotes and a text button under the main button.
- **The Pro graphic:** a soft pink box with the placeholder logo in the
  corner, which wraps the line early ("…10 jobs a day / for you."). The
  logo is a placeholder until the owner commissions one, so the Pro graphic
  shouldn't lean on it at all.

## Dribbble

Searches: "paywall ios", "premium subscription app", "upgrade to pro
mobile", "membership card app", "ticket pass ui", "skeuomorphic membership
card".

- **Most paywall shots are one cliché:** dark gradients with a gem, crown or
  glowing orb, five checkmarks and a "Save 50%" tag (for example "Upgrade
  Pro Screen", "Cleaner Pro", "Paywall for Beauty App"). They read as
  generic and loud; nothing there suits Pinkslip.
- **The light, calm ones are rarer:** "AI Chat Pro Upgrade Flow" (a soft
  hero, Monthly and Annual cards, a "remind me before the trial ends"
  switch).
- **What fits Pinkslip is the printed member card:**
  - "Snail Mail Social Club Membership Card": pink, letterpress, a stamped
    mark, lines for name, member since and number, and a short creed on
    the back.
  - "Speakeasy Membership Cards" and the vintage scrip: serial numbers, a
    signature line, rules and borders.
  - A VIP pass with half-notches on its sides, like a ticket's
    perforation, and a rubber stamp across it.
  - "1Pass Fitness Plans": plans drawn as passes and coupons with stubs.

  These are the same family as Pinkslip's paper objects (the ticket, the
  torn stub, the stamps, the punch card), and they're crafted rather than
  decorated.

## Reading

- **The honest paywall** (Blinkist, reported by RevenueCat): showing the
  trial as a timeline, with a promise to remind before it ends, raised
  trial starts about 23% and cut complaints 55%. The figures are Blinkist's
  own, so treat them as direction. Trust decides whether a trial converts.
- **RevenueCat, "How top apps approach paywalls":**
  - Keep it to one choice.
  - Speak to what the person was just doing.
  - Be transparent, with no spin.
  - Show how to cancel.
  - After buying, confirm it and point to the first thing to do.
  - Avoid dense legal walls and dark patterns.
  - Don't show the paywall so often that it feels like spam.
- **App Review (3.1.2):**
  - **What the screen needs:** the subscription's title, length and price,
    and working links to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
  - **The billed price must be the clearest price element.** Apps are
    rejected when "$3.46 a week" outshines "$14.99 a month".
  - **Restore** is expected.
- **Ours, already decided** (`pricing-models-2026-10-24.md`): Pro ends when
  you're hired, and the Plan page says so up front. The audience is short on
  money and anxious, so the screen's job is to feel safe, not urgent.

## What it means for Pinkslip

1. **No logo on the Pro graphic.** Make Pro's object out of type, paper and
   a stamp, so it holds up whatever the logo becomes.
2. **Use the objects already designed:**
   - The punch card (`PunchCard`, grain, holes) is listed in the system as
     Plan's usage object; Free's 3 a week are three holes.
   - The stamp's land motion can mark the moment you go Pro.
3. **One surface for the answer, the rest on the page.** One left edge for
   everything on the page (16), and the same inner edge (16) for everything
   inside a card.
4. **The sheet is honest and short.**
   - Lead with one promise.
   - Then the timeline (today; a reminder the day before it ends; when the
     charge starts; it ends when you're hired).
   - Then the two plans, the button, and one line of terms.
5. **Copy:** the promise is said once, with no "Save x%", no badges and no
   countdowns.

## What was picked (round 6)

The owner approved Paper "Plan and Pro · round 6 · Oct 24":

- **The Get Pro sheet:**
  - A plum-to-pink field across the top, with a white headline and white dots for Free's 3 and Pro's 70.
  - A white panel rises over the field with the Monthly/Weekly switch, an ink button, one line of terms, and Terms · Privacy.
  - The rows read "3 applications per week" and "70 applications per week".
  - Restore is set in Medium.
- **Plan on Free and Plan on Pro:** round 4's first layout.
  - The big Founders count with its dots, the week grid, and the subscription card.
  - The mono capitals became plain Untitled labels ("Free plan", "This week").
  - The Free page's Pro card stays near-black with pink dots. The owner tried the sheet's gradient on it and found it cheap.
- **Copy says "per":** "per day" and "per week" throughout.
