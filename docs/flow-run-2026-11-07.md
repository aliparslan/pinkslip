## Your questions

- **1.5, jobs fading in then sliding left.** Intended: rows that fit rise in,
  rows that no longer fit leave to the side.
- **2.4, everything has a pink dot.** The dot means you haven't opened the
  job. There's no separate "fresh" mark; new jobs sit under New today.
- **2.15, "why should it show?"** The Search tab says "Search every open job
  and company, not just your feed", but it only searches jobs that fit your
  search. Either the words change, or Search looks at every job (a Worker
  change and a deploy). Your call.
- **2.20, careers site full screen.** It opens Safari's full-screen view,
  the default. Terms and Privacy open as a sheet; the careers site can too.
- **2.21, People always empty.** People are admin-only for now, so a guest
  (and anyone without outreach) sees an empty tab. It could be hidden for
  them.
- **2.26, text not highlightable.** Paragraphs without a link select a range
  in pink. A paragraph with a link can only be copied whole: iOS's text view
  can't tell which word a tap landed on, so links and range selection don't
  mix (the Oct 24 trade-off). Headings don't select.
- **2.43, "Did you apply?" takes a moment.** It waits for the browser to
  finish closing; iOS won't open a second sheet while one is still leaving.
- **5.5, a dialog for Skills.** It's iOS's text prompt for a group's name.
  The only other one is Outreach's signature. It could be an inline field
  instead.
- **16.2.** Dismissing the sign-in sheet landing on Jobs, signed out, is
  intended. Kit stays because it follows the phone (owner builds), not the
  account.
