# Redesign brief (2026-10-11)

Drafted from the decisions in [redesign-2026-10.md](redesign-2026-10.md) and
approved by the owner on 2026-10-11. The owner will refine it as the designs
take shape.

## Feeling

Helpful, useful, convenient, beautiful, intuitive.

## Who it's for and the moment they use it

- **Who:**
  - Interns, new grads, and people one to five years in.
  - Mostly US students, mostly on iPhone.
- **When:**
  - Between classes or in bed, a few minutes at a time.
  - They check what's new, apply to one or two jobs, and see whether anything
    came back.
- **How they feel:** anxious, short on time, and tired of spreadsheets.

The app should answer "is anything happening?" at a glance, and make the next
step obvious.

## Love

Each reference is listed with why it's here.

| Reference | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Apple Mail, inbox rows | Trailing age, a leading unread dot, and a title that carries the row. The model for the job row |
| Things 3 | Calm spacing. Completing something feels good without being loud |
| Flighty | Paper-ticket details, numbers you can read at a glance, a status timeline, and the best Live Activity on iOS. The model for "paper moments" and the apply queue |
| Linear (iOS) | Status glyphs that fill as work advances. The model for Tracker stages |
| Mercury, application status | A vertical timeline that makes waiting feel like progress. The model for an application's page |
| Apple Wallet passes | A real-object metaphor (perforations, a stub) kept restrained |
| Klim's specimen pages | Founders Grotesk and Untitled Sans at their best |

## Don't want

- **The direct competitor's look.** We won't look at their designs at all, so
  nothing is copied by accident.
- **Generic AI-product styling:**
  - purple-blue gradients
  - sparkle icons on everything
  - glowing "AI" badges
- **Busy forms:** a descriptor under every field, boxed inputs with both a
  fill and a border.
- **System red for anything that isn't an error.**
- **Walls of heavy black chips.**

## Keep from today

- The pink: one hue with two roles, ink and fill.
- The two-slips mark idea, until the commissioned logo arrives.
- Warm neutrals and the dark mode.
- Apple's native chrome.
- The calm row.
- The motion from the craft pass.

## Change from today

Everything agreed in the assessment:
- **Row:** one row anatomy on both platforms, with semibold titles, the
  company first, and a trailing age.
- **Grid:** two keylines (16 and 60pt).
- **Type:** iOS text-style proportions; five sizes in daily use.
- **Forms:** grouped lists with trailing values.
- **Onboarding:** a welcome first, and the right primary actions.
- **Job page:** facts first, and a sticky Save and Apply bar.
- **Navigation:** Jobs · Tracker · You.

## Platforms

iOS first. Mobile web and desktop get their own designs afterwards.

## Constraints

- **Fonts:** Klim trial files for now. Buy the app licence before any App
  Store build.
- **Logo:** a placeholder until it's commissioned. No wordmark.
- **Accessibility:**
  - Dynamic Type, WCAG AA contrast, VoiceOver labels.
  - Reduce Motion respected.
  - 44pt targets.
- **Budget and dates:** $0 budget. No launch date; the App Store is on hold.
