# Research

What the owner shares (courses, articles, sites, people) and what came of
it. The folder follows Karpathy's "LLM Wiki" pattern: sources are read
once and folded into notes that are kept current, so later sessions don't
re-derive them. Read this index first.

- **[directions.md](directions.md)**: every direction found so far, with
  where it came from, what it needs and its status. Start here for "what
  next".
- **[people.md](people.md)**: people worth following, with proof of work.
- **[reading.md](reading.md)**: every article and tool read, what it says
  and what it means here, marked read, partly, secondhand or unread.
- **[log.md](log.md)**: what was added when.

## How to keep it

- **Ingest.** When the owner shares something, read all of it. Write or
  update the source's notes. Add rows to `directions.md` with their source.
  Update `people.md` and `reading.md`. Add a line to `log.md`.
- **Query.** A useful answer worked out in chat gets filed back here as a
  page or a row.
- **Lint, now and then.** Check for directions shipped or dropped but
  still marked proposed, notes that newer work has made wrong, and
  sources marked unread.
- **When the owner approves a direction**, move it to the design roadmap
  (or `ROADMAP.md`) and mark it there.
