## Description

Job hunting shouldn't be another job.

Pinkslip keeps the whole search in one calm place, so you spend less time
hunting and more time on the jobs worth it.

Find
Pinkslip checks company career pages every hour and brings you new grad and
early-career roles straight from the source. Say what you're looking for
once, and your feed shows only what fits.

Hear first
Turn on alerts and know about a new posting as soon as it fits your search.

Apply
Add your resume once. When you apply, Pinkslip opens the employer's
application and fills in what it already knows. You check it and send it.

Track
Every application in one list. Forward job emails to your Pinkslip address
and replies move each one along for you.

No ads, and Pinkslip never sells your information. Look around as a guest,
or sign in with Apple, Google or your email to keep everything in sync.
