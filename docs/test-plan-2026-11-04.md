# Full test plan (Nov 4, 2026)

Every flow a person can take through Pinkslip, so the owner can go through
them in one sitting and mark what breaks. Each flow is steps, then what
should happen. Flows marked **new** cover the Nov 4 fixes; work through those
first.

How to use it:
- Tick `[x]` when a flow behaves as written. Put a short note under any flow
  that doesn't ("Fill again: bar never changed"), and a screenshot path if
  you have one.
- Use your phone on the owner channel for iOS, and Safari on a laptop for
  the website. "Guest" means signed out.
- Never press Submit on a real employer's form unless you mean to apply.
  Use the Kit samples where a flow says so.
- Flows 7.x and 8.x send real pushes and emails. Run them on your own account.

What the machine already covers, so you don't have to (`bun run check`,
`bun test`, `bun run test:e2e`, the native Playwright suite): form reading
and filling on Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby markup; the matcher; location
rules; the send rules; alerts timing; the API routes. What it can't cover:
anything on a signed phone (push buttons, Apple sign-in, OTA updates, the
keyboard), real employer pages, and whether a screen looks right.

## 0. New on Nov 4: check these first

- [ ] **0.1 Fill again (new).** Open a job with an Ashby or Greenhouse form →
  Apply → let it fill → clear a field it filled → ⋯ → Fill again. The bar
  shows "Reading…" then "Filling…", and the field fills back in.
- [ ] **0.2 Fill again with nothing left (new).** On a form where only
  questions for you remain, ⋯ → Fill again. The bar flashes "Reading…" and
  "Filling…", then shows "N need you" again; nothing else changes.
- [ ] **0.3 Ashby knows you applied (new).** Apply on an Ashby form you
  really want, press its Submit. Within about 2 seconds of Ashby's "Success"
  box, the bar goes away and the job is marked applied ("Applied to X. It's
  in Tracker."). No "Did that go through?".
- [ ] **0.4 No second ask (new).** On any form, press Submit where no
  confirmation shows, wait for "Did that go through?", tap I applied. The
  browser closes, the job is applied, and "Did you apply?" does **not**
  open after it.
- [ ] **0.5 Ask when it doesn't know.** Open a form, fill nothing, close
  the browser. "Did you apply?" opens once.
- [ ] **0.6 Contact fields above the keyboard (new).** You → Resume →
  Contact → tap Website (the last field). The page scrolls so the field
  sits above the keyboard. Same for GitHub and LinkedIn.
- [ ] **0.7 Long pages above the keyboard (new).** You → Resume →
  Experience → a role → tap the description. You → Answers → the last
  answer. Each field stays visible while typing.
- [ ] **0.8 Pages with a bottom button (check).** You → Help and feedback →
  type in the message. You → Resume → Skills → the last group. The field
  stays visible and isn't hidden behind the Send / Add a group button. If it
  is, note which page.
- [ ] **0.9 Typing in a form in the in-app browser.** In an application
  form, tap a field near the bottom (phone, city, a long question). The
  field stays visible above the keyboard and the "From your answers" strip.
  If the strip covers it, note the field.
- [ ] **0.10 No foreign jobs (new, after deploy).** Jobs and today's alert
  show no Bangalore, Toronto or other non-US roles. A job listed in both
  Bengaluru and Austin still shows.

## 1. First run and sign-in (iOS)

- [ ] **1.1 Fresh install.** Delete the app, install from TestFlight, open
  it. The hook screen ("Job hunting shouldn't be another job."), then "What
  Pinkslip handles", then the chip questions.
- [ ] **1.2 Chip questions.** Answer each question; Back keeps answers.
  Finishing shows Jobs filtered by your answers.
- [ ] **1.3 Guest browse.** Without signing in: scroll Jobs, open a job,
  open a company. Saving or applying asks you to sign in.
- [ ] **1.4 Sign in with Apple.** You → sign in → Apple. You land where you
  were, signed in; your name shows on You.
- [ ] **1.5 Sign in with email.** Sign out → email → enter the code from the
  email. The link in the email also works (opens the app).
- [ ] **1.6 Sign out and back in.** Sign out, sign back in. Saved jobs,
  resume and settings are still there.
- [ ] **1.7 Delete account.** On a spare account only: You → Account →
  Delete. You're signed out and that account can't sign back in to old data.

## 2. Jobs

- [ ] **2.1 Feed loads.** Jobs shows rows with company, title, place, pay
  where known. Pull to refresh works.
- [ ] **2.2 Only your search.** Every row fits Your search (level, field,
  place). Note any that don't, with the job's link.
- [ ] **2.3 Swipe right to save, left to hide.** Each shows its toast with
  Undo; Undo brings the row back.
- [ ] **2.4 Swipe to mark applied.** The row moves to Library → Tracker.
- [ ] **2.5 Job page.** Open a job: title, company, place, pay, the
  description with selectable text, Apply.
- [ ] **2.6 Job page ⋯ menu.** Save, Hide, I applied, Email recruiter (if
  on), Tailor resume (wrench page), Hide company, Report listing. Each does
  what it says; Hide company removes all that company's jobs with Undo.
- [ ] **2.7 Share.** Share opens the share sheet with the job's link; the
  link opens the job on the website.
- [ ] **2.8 Report listing.** Report as closed: "Report sent" and the job
  leaves your feed.
- [ ] **2.9 Company page.** Open a company: its open jobs, and the people
  count if recruiters are on for you.
- [ ] **2.10 Closed job.** Open a job that has closed. It says so and Apply
  isn't offered as if it were open.

## 3. Applying without auto-apply

- [ ] **3.1 Safari apply.** With auto-apply off for your account (or as a
  guest who signed in): Apply → the posting opens in Safari over the app →
  close it. "Did you apply?" asks; Yes marks it applied and it tears into
  Tracker.
- [ ] **3.2 Not yet.** Same, answer Not yet. Nothing is marked.

## 4. Applying in the in-app browser (assisted)

Use the Kit sample first (You → Kit → the apply sample), then one real form
per board you see most (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby).

- [ ] **4.1 Opens and fills.** Apply → the prep sheet → the browser opens
  with "Apply at X", the board's host under it. "Filling the form 3 of 9"
  counts up.
- [ ] **4.2 What it filled.** Fields Pinkslip filled have a soft pink tint;
  the ones that need you are outlined in pink.
- [ ] **4.3 Arrows.** "N need you" with up/down arrows jumps to each blank
  in turn and wraps around.
- [ ] **4.4 Resume attached.** The resume field holds your resume's file
  name.
- [ ] **4.5 Your answers strip.** Tap a blank question you've answered
  before. "From your answers" shows above the keyboard; tapping it puts the
  answer in.
- [ ] **4.6 Learns what you type.** Type an answer to a new question, close
  the browser. You → Answers lists it.
- [ ] **4.7 All filled.** When nothing needs you: "All N filled · Submit is
  at the bottom" and Go to Submit scrolls there and highlights the button.
- [ ] **4.8 Greenhouse applied.** Submit on Greenhouse: the confirmation
  page marks it applied with no question.
- [ ] **4.9 Lever applied.** Same on Lever.
- [ ] **4.10 Ashby applied.** See 0.3.
- [ ] **4.11 Did that go through?** On a board with no confirmation page,
  after Submit the bar asks; I applied marks it (see 0.4).
- [ ] **4.12 Open in Safari.** ⋯ → Open in Safari opens the same page.
- [ ] **4.13 Close mid-fill.** Close while it's filling. The sheet goes; no
  error toast; the job isn't marked.
- [ ] **4.14 Navigating.** Follow a link inside the form (e.g. a privacy
  policy) and come back. It reads the form again rather than filling the
  wrong page.
- [ ] **4.15 Phone country and combobox fields.** Note any dropdown or
  search field that stays empty or gets the wrong value (the phone
  "Country" on Ashby has stopped sends before).

## 5. Library

- [ ] **5.1 Saved.** Saved jobs show newest first; unsave from a row.
- [ ] **5.2 Tracker.** Applied jobs show with their stage; open one to see
  the application page.
- [ ] **5.3 Change a stage.** Move an application to Interviewing, then to
  Offer or Rejected. The date it changed shows.
- [ ] **5.4 Mark not applied.** From the application or the job page: I
  didn't apply. It leaves Tracker.
- [ ] **5.5 Email-detected updates.** With email tracking on, an employer
  email ("we received your application", an interview invite) shows on the
  right application.
- [ ] **5.6 "Which application is this?".** When an email can't be matched,
  the push asks once. Pick the job; the email moves there. Note it if the
  same company asks again on later days.

## 6. You

- [ ] **6.1 Your search.** Change level, field and places; Jobs updates to
  match.
- [ ] **6.2 Alerts.** Turn alerts on and off; change the daily alert time.
  The next alert comes at that time.
- [ ] **6.3 Email tracking.** Connect, see email history, disconnect.
- [ ] **6.4 Resume import.** Resume → import a PDF (try a LaTeX one). "Use
  this resume?" lists what it found; Use this fills the sections; contact
  only fills empty fields.
- [ ] **6.5 Resume sections.** Add, edit and delete an Experience entry, a
  school, a project, a skills group, a certification. Order is newest first.
- [ ] **6.6 Answers.** Add, edit and delete an answer.
- [ ] **6.7 Hidden companies.** You → Auto-apply → Hidden companies, or the
  company list. Unhide one; its jobs come back.
- [ ] **6.8 Plan.** Plan shows Free or Pro and the allowances ("3 a week",
  "Up to 10 a day").
- [ ] **6.9 Auto-apply settings.** Ask me / Apply for me (Pro) / Off. Pause
  → For a day shows "Paused until …" with Resume; Resume clears it.
- [ ] **6.10 Appearance.** System / Light / Dark switch at once and stick
  after relaunch.
- [ ] **6.11 Help and feedback.** Send a message; it arrives at support.
  The reply address isn't your personal one.
- [ ] **6.12 What's new.** Lists the latest notes.
- [ ] **6.13 Support Pinkslip.** Opens; nothing charges without confirming.
- [ ] **6.14 Tailoring.** Shows the wrench "coming soon" page (intended).

## 7. Asks and auto-apply (owner account; real pushes)

- [ ] **7.1 A strong match asks.** When a strong match posts in your
  search (8am–10pm), a push "Apply for you?" with the job, and "Hold to
  apply".
- [ ] **7.2 Hold → Apply for me.** Hold the push, Apply for me. The send
  starts; You → the send's page shows it running, then Sent or a question
  for you.
- [ ] **7.3 Hold → Not this one.** The job is hidden and doesn't ask again.
- [ ] **7.4 Tap the push.** Opens the job.
- [ ] **7.5 Quiet hours.** Nothing asks between 10pm and 8am.
- [ ] **7.6 Paused.** Pause for a day; no asks or sends until it ends.
- [ ] **7.7 A send that needs you.** When a required question has no saved
  answer, one push lists all of them at once; answering them finishes the
  send.
- [ ] **7.8 Sent page.** A finished send shows the confirmation shot and
  the answers it gave. "Sent, but no confirmation yet" should now be rare on
  Ashby (fixed Nov 4); note any that still say it.

## 8. Recruiters and outreach (where on)

- [ ] **8.1 People at a company.** Company page shows the people count;
  open a person.
- [ ] **8.2 Write to a recruiter.** Email recruiter → the draft → Send. It
  goes from your connected address; the follow-ups are scheduled.
- [ ] **8.3 A reply.** A recruiter's reply shows on the person's page and
  stops the follow-ups.
- [ ] **8.4 Did you apply? from a person.** The O4 sheet asks and marks the
  job.

## 9. Notifications (iOS)

- [ ] **9.1 Permission.** First alert setup asks for notifications once.
- [ ] **9.2 Daily alert.** Arrives at your time, lists new matches; tapping
  opens Jobs on them.
- [ ] **9.3 Badge.** The app badge clears when you've seen them.
- [ ] **9.4 No duplicates.** The same job never alerts twice.

## 10. Website (Safari, laptop and phone)

- [ ] **10.1 Home as guest.** pinkslip.work loads Jobs; the icon rail on wide

- [ ] **10.2 Sign in.** Apple and email sign-in work and land you back where
  you were.
- [ ] **10.3 Jobs.** Same checks as 2.1–2.7.
- [ ] **10.4 Library and You.** Saved, Tracker and settings match the app.
- [ ] **10.5 A shared job link.** Opens the job for a guest.
- [ ] **10.6 Phone width.** No sideways scroll; tapping a field doesn't zoom

- [ ] **10.7 Privacy and terms pages.** Load, including the Recruiters

- [ ] **10.8 Add to home screen.** The icon is the App Store icon.

- [ ] **11.1 Over-the-air update.** After a publish, close and reopen the

- [ ] **11.2 Update notes.** What's new shows the note.
