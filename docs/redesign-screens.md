# Every screen to design (2026-10-11)

The owner wants the whole direction designed in Paper before any building:
every screen the roadmap needs, in Take A (Quiet), light and dark. This file
is the checklist. [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) has the phases, and
[redesign-wireframes.md](redesign-wireframes.md) has the decisions so far.

## What's on the roadmap

- **Auto-apply:**
  - Level 1: assisted fill in the in-app browser.
  - Level 2: a queue you approve, with a Live Activity.
  - Level 3: autonomous applying, with Ask first before Automatic.
- **Recruiter outreach:**
  - Finding recruiters, from the owner's own lists.
  - Cold-email templates.
  - Follow-ups after 3 days and then a week.
  - Daily email quotas.
  - An outreach log.
  - An email connection.
  - Company pages.
- **Tracking from email:**
  - Forwarded emails move applications along.
  - Stats, CSV export, and the flow diagram (Sankey).
- **Events:** a board of webinars and recruiting events, with community
  submissions that the admin reviews.
- **Writing:** what's new (changes and notices), and tips and guides.
- **Pricing:** usage-based Free and Pro, to cover costs.
- **Also agreed:**
  - A Today tab once background applying ships.
  - The onboarding redesign.
  - Alert settings.
  - One saved search in place of Preferences and Filters.

## Batch 1 decisions

- **Onboarding:**
  - Five steps: welcome, career stage, roles and places, resume, alerts.
  - Continue sits in the same place on every step, and the bar at the top
    shows progress.
  - The welcome has the two-slips mark, one sentence, and a preview of real
    rows with an Applied stamp. No wordmark.
  - Career stage allows several choices, each with a short definition.
  - Roles are six chips at most. Cities open a picker, and Remote and visa
    sponsorship are switches.
  - On the resume and alert steps, the main button is the action ("Import
    PDF", "Turn on alerts"), with Skip or Not now under it.
- **Edit search:**
  - A page sheet, headed by the live match count ("48 a week") and one line
    saying the search drives the feed, alerts and auto-apply.
  - Grouped rows with values on the right.
- **Full job page:**
  - Requirements follow About the role.
  - Benefits, About the company and Equal opportunity are collapsed.
  - The company card closes the page: Follow, open roles, people, events.
- **The ⋯ menu:** I applied, Not for me, Hide all of a company's jobs, Open
  original posting, then Report a problem, set apart.
- **Search:**
  - One field for jobs, companies and events.
  - Scope chips with counts.
  - A company result comes first, with Follow.
- **Notifications:**
  - Grouped by new jobs, your applications, and outreach and events.
  - Quiet hours sit apart.
  - Needs you is the only time-sensitive alert.
- **Answers:**
  - Common questions are filled in for you.
  - Work authorization, sponsorship and demographic questions are always
    asked on the form, never answered for you. This follows the existing
    rule against answering legal and status questions automatically.
- **Empty states:**
  - **An empty Tracker:** the paper-slips drawing, one line, Browse jobs,
    and "Track one from an email".
  - **No new jobs today:** a card with one concrete suggestion ("Add
    Austin") and Edit search. The rest of the week's list shows below it.
- **Offline:** not drawn. The plan is a one-line banner under the title, with
  the time of the last refresh.

## Batch 2 decisions (tracking)

On the Paper page "Batch 2 · Tracking".

- **The application page:**
  - The job header, then the stage glyph with "Change".
  - A Next card: the interview date, Add to Calendar, and the interview
    guide (guides appear in context).
  - The timeline runs oldest to newest. Past steps are filled dots and
    upcoming steps are hollow.
  - An email-detected step shows the subject line it came from, and "Not
    right?" to correct it.
  - Notes, people at the company, and the posting close the page.
- **Changing the stage:**
  - A sheet listing Saved, Applied, Assessment, Interview and Offer, then
    "Ended" with Rejected, Withdrew and No response.
  - The glyphs fill as the stages advance. Green is only for an offer, and
    endings are grey.
- **Email tracking:**
  - The personal forwarding address, in mono, with Copy and "Set up
    auto-forward".
  - A "Recently filed" list. An email that couldn't be matched asks you to
    choose a job.
  - An optional switch: "Ask before moving a stage".
- **Stats:**
  - Season, 30 days, or all time.
  - Four big numbers: applied, heard back, interviews, offers.
  - The flow chart, with "Share as image" and "Export CSV".
  - Response rate and time to first reply, split by who applied: you,
    Pinkslip, or after outreach.
- **The lock screen:**
  - A Needs-you question answered from the notification. The answer is saved
    to the answers bank.
  - An interview-invite notice.
  - The morning digest.

## Batch 3 decisions (auto-apply)

On the Paper page "Batch 3 · Auto-apply".

- **Level 1, assisted fill:**
  - The form opens straight away in the in-app browser.
  - Fields Pinkslip filled are tinted pale pink.
  - Blanks that need you get a pink outline.
  - A native bar at the bottom shows "15 of 17 filled · 2 need you", with
    up and down arrows to jump between blanks, and Done.
- **Level 2, select mode:**
  - Jobs gets selection circles, and the title becomes "3 selected".
  - Jobs you've already applied to are dimmed.
  - The bar holds "Save all" and "Apply to 3".
- **Level 2, the review sheet:**
  - Each job's readiness: Ready, or what it still needs (in pink).
  - Questions asked by every job are answered once.
  - Legal questions are always yours. Start stays off ("Answer 2 to
    start") until they're answered.
- **Level 2, the Live Activity:**
  - On the lock screen: the current company, "2/3", a segmented progress
    bar, and an Answer button when something needs you.
  - In the Dynamic Island, compact: the mark and "2/3". Expanded: the
    question, with Skip and Answer.
- **Level 3, settings:**
  - Off, Ask first or Automatic.
  - Rules, written as one sentence that starts "Only jobs that":
    - match strongly
    - are at companies you follow
    - were posted within 3 days
    - need only answers we have
  - A daily limit.
  - "Pause for a week".
- **Level 3, the Ask first list:** "4 for today", all pre-selected, sent with
  one tap ("Send 4"). There's also "Not today".
- **Level 3, the receipt:** "What we sent" splits what was filled from your
  profile from what you answered, and lets you withdraw.
- **The Today tab:**
  - A summary of yesterday: applied, replies, interviews, new jobs.
  - Needs you, which includes follow-ups that are due.
  - The batch card, then New for you.
  - A four-item tab bar: Today, Jobs, Tracker, You.
