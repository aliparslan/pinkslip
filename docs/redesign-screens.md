# Every screen to design (2026-10-11)

The owner wants the whole direction designed in Paper before any building:
every screen the roadmap needs, in Take A (Quiet), light and dark. This file
is the checklist. [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) has the phases, and
[redesign-wireframes.md](redesign-wireframes.md) has the decisions so far.

## What's on the roadmap

- **Auto-apply:**
  - Level 1: assisted fill in the in-app browser.
  - Level 2: a queue you approve, with a Live Activity.
  - Level 3: autonomous applying, with Ask first before Automatic.
- **Recruiter outreach:**
  - Finding recruiters, from the owner's own lists.
  - Cold-email templates.
  - Follow-ups after 3 days and then a week.
  - Daily email quotas.
  - An outreach log.
  - An email connection.
  - Company pages.
- **Tracking from email:**
  - Forwarded emails move applications along.
  - Stats, CSV export, and the flow diagram (Sankey).
- **Events:** a board of webinars and recruiting events, with community
  submissions that the admin reviews.
- **Writing:** what's new (changes and notices), and tips and guides.
- **Pricing:** usage-based Free and Pro, to cover costs.
- **Also agreed:**
  - A Today tab once background applying ships.
  - The onboarding redesign.
  - Alert settings.
  - One saved search in place of Preferences and Filters.

## Batch 1 decisions

- **Onboarding:**
  - Five steps: welcome, career stage, roles and places, resume, alerts.
  - Continue sits in the same place on every step, and the bar at the top
    shows progress.
  - The welcome has the two-slips mark, one sentence, and a preview of real
    rows with an Applied stamp. No wordmark.
  - Career stage allows several choices, each with a short definition.
  - Roles are six chips at most. Cities open a picker, and Remote and visa
    sponsorship are switches.
  - On the resume and alert steps, the main button is the action ("Import
    PDF", "Turn on alerts"), with Skip or Not now under it.
- **Edit search:**
  - A page sheet, headed by the live match count ("48 a week") and one line
    saying the search drives the feed, alerts and auto-apply.
  - Grouped rows with values on the right.
- **Full job page:**
  - Requirements follow About the role.
  - Benefits, About the company and Equal opportunity are collapsed.
  - The company card closes the page: Follow, open roles, people, events.
- **The ⋯ menu:** I applied, Not for me, Hide all of a company's jobs, Open
  original posting, then Report a problem, set apart.
- **Search:**
  - One field for jobs, companies and events.
  - Scope chips with counts.
  - A company result comes first, with Follow.
- **Notifications:**
  - Grouped by new jobs, your applications, and outreach and events.
  - Quiet hours sit apart.
  - Needs you is the only time-sensitive alert.
- **Answers:**
  - Common questions are filled in for you.
  - Work authorization, sponsorship and demographic questions are always
    asked on the form, never answered for you. This follows the existing
    rule against answering legal and status questions automatically.
- **Empty states:**
  - **An empty Tracker:** the paper-slips drawing, one line, Browse jobs,
    and "Track one from an email".
  - **No new jobs today:** a card with one concrete suggestion ("Add
    Austin") and Edit search. The rest of the week's list shows below it.
- **Offline:** not drawn. The plan is a one-line banner under the title, with
  the time of the last refresh.

## Batch 2 decisions (tracking)

On the Paper page "Batch 2 · Tracking".

- **The application page:**
  - The job header, then the stage glyph with "Change".
  - A Next card: the interview date, Add to Calendar, and the interview
    guide (guides appear in context).
  - The timeline runs oldest to newest. Past steps are filled dots and
    upcoming steps are hollow.
  - An email-detected step shows the subject line it came from, and "Not
    right?" to correct it.
  - Notes, people at the company, and the posting close the page.
- **Changing the stage:**
  - A sheet listing Saved, Applied, Assessment, Interview and Offer, then
    "Ended" with Rejected, Withdrew and No response.
  - The glyphs fill as the stages advance. Green is only for an offer, and
    endings are grey.
- **Email tracking:**
  - The personal forwarding address, in mono, with Copy and "Set up
    auto-forward".
  - A "Recently filed" list. An email that couldn't be matched asks you to
    choose a job.
  - An optional switch: "Ask before moving a stage".
- **Stats:**
  - Season, 30 days, or all time.
  - Four big numbers: applied, heard back, interviews, offers.
  - The flow chart, with "Share as image" and "Export CSV".
  - Response rate and time to first reply, split by who applied: you,
    Pinkslip, or after outreach.
- **The lock screen:**
  - A Needs-you question answered from the notification. The answer is saved
    to the answers bank.
  - An interview-invite notice.
  - The morning digest.

## Batch 3 decisions (auto-apply)

On the Paper page "Batch 3 · Auto-apply".

- **Level 1, assisted fill:**
  - The form opens straight away in the in-app browser.
  - Fields Pinkslip filled are tinted pale pink.
  - Blanks that need you get a pink outline.
  - A native bar at the bottom shows "15 of 17 filled · 2 need you", with
    up and down arrows to jump between blanks, and Done.
- **Level 2, select mode:**
  - Jobs gets selection circles, and the title becomes "3 selected".
  - Jobs you've already applied to are dimmed.
  - The bar holds "Save all" and "Apply to 3".
- **Level 2, the review sheet:**
  - Each job's readiness: Ready, or what it still needs (in pink).
  - Questions asked by every job are answered once.
  - Legal questions are always yours. Start stays off ("Answer 2 to
    start") until they're answered.
- **Level 2, the Live Activity:**
  - On the lock screen: the current company, "2/3", a segmented progress
    bar, and an Answer button when something needs you.
  - In the Dynamic Island, compact: the mark and "2/3". Expanded: the
    question, with Skip and Answer.
- **Level 3, settings:**
  - Off, Ask first or Automatic.
  - Rules, written as one sentence that starts "Only jobs that":
    - match strongly
    - are at companies you follow
    - were posted within 3 days
    - need only answers we have
  - A daily limit.
  - "Pause for a week".
- **Level 3, the Ask first list:** "4 for today", all pre-selected, sent with
  one tap ("Send 4"). There's also "Not today".
- **Level 3, the receipt:** "What we sent" splits what was filled from your
  profile from what you answered, and lets you withdraw.
- **The Today tab:**
  - A summary of yesterday: applied, replies, interviews, new jobs.
  - Needs you, which includes follow-ups that are due.
  - The batch card, then New for you.
  - A four-item tab bar: Today, Jobs, Tracker, You.

## Batch 4 decisions (outreach)

On the Paper page "Batch 4 · Outreach".

- **The company page:**
  - Logo, name and one line of facts.
  - Following (or Follow) and Careers site.
  - Segments for Jobs, People and Events, with counts.
  - Each person shows their role, whether the email has been checked, and
    where the contact came from ("from Pinkslip's list").
  - "Write to …" is pinned to the bottom.
  - A separate person page isn't needed yet. The row and the conversation
    page cover it.
- **The composer:**
  - A page sheet with To and Subject, then template chips: Just applied,
    Asking about roles, After an event, Referral.
  - Personalized words are highlighted in pale pink.
  - A follow-up plan ("Oct 14, then Oct 21", set to remind you) and Send
    from (Mail app or Gmail).
- **Tracker's Outreach side:** Follow-up due (pink), Waiting and Replied. Each
  row shows company, person and the next step.
- **The conversation page:**
  - A due card ("Review and send", "Skip").
  - A log of what you wrote and the planned follow-ups.
  - Follow-ups stop as soon as the person replies.
- **Outreach settings:**
  - Send from Gmail (sending only) or the Mail app. Pinkslip never reads
    the inbox, and replies arrive through the forwarding address.
  - Follow-ups: remind me or send for me.
  - Timing: 3 days, then a week.
  - Up to 5 emails a day, to people at jobs you apply to.
  - Emails go out on weekday mornings in the recipient's time zone.

## Batch 5 decisions (events and writing)

On the Paper page "Batch 5 · Events and writing".

- **Events:**
  - The large title, with a "+" to share an event.
  - Filter chips: For you, Online, Near me, Companies I follow.
  - Rows grouped by week, each with a date block, host, title, and time in
    your time zone.
  - Events you registered for say so. Community events say "Shared by a
    student".
- **The event page:**
  - It reuses the job page and its ticket: date, time, where, who it's for,
    and length. The stub holds the host and when registration closes.
  - The bar holds Remind me and Register, which opens the host's page.
  - Add to Calendar moves into the ⋯ menu, and appears after you register.
- **Sharing an event:**
  - Paste a link and the details fill in.
  - Every event is checked before it shows up, through the admin review
    queue.
- **What's new:**
  - Updates and Guides segments.
  - Each post shows a date, a Founders title and one line.
- **The release sheet:** shown once after a big release. The mark, a title,
  three features, and Continue.
- **A guide:**
  - A reading page: a read-time line, a big title, short steps.
  - A button at the end opens the composer. Guides appear in context, so
    this one also opens from the composer.

## Batch 6 decisions (pricing)

On the Paper page "Batch 6 · Pricing".

- **The prices are placeholders.** They are the lean from
  [redesign-2026-10.md](redesign-2026-10.md): one Pro plan at $9.99 a week,
  $24.99 a month or $59.99 a season (3 months). The model isn't decided yet.
- **The Plan page:**
  - First, this week's usage: "Applied for you 3 of 5" and "Emails sent for
    you 1 of 5", each with a meter.
  - One line says what stays free: jobs, alerts, the tracker and filling
    forms yourself.
  - Then the Pro card: four things it adds, the three billing periods (the
    selected one in pale pink), and "Try Pro free for 7 days".
- **The paywall at the moment of intent:**
  - It appears only when an action goes past the free allowance. Here that's
    selecting 3 jobs after the week's 5 are used.
  - It's a sheet over the screen you were on, so the selection survives.
  - The title says what happened: "You've used this week's 5". One line
    gives the reason to upgrade.
  - "Apply to these 3 myself" is always there as a way out, and the price
    sits in the footer.

## Batch 7 decisions (admin)

On the Paper page "Batch 7 · Admin". Admin is reached from You, and only
admins see it.

- **Admin home:** a grouped list.
  - To review: Inbox, Shared events and Jev comparisons. Counts are pink
    only when something is waiting on the admin.
  - Pipeline: Runs (the last run's age), Sources (how many need a look) and
    Product health.
  - Import contacts, with the total.
- **Inbox:**
  - One screen with three tabs: flagged jobs, reports and feedback, each
    with a count.
  - A flagged job uses the job-row header. Under it, why it was flagged,
    one reason per line ("Rules and Jev disagree on level"). Then Posting,
    Hide and Show.
- **Shared events:**
  - Tabs for New, Approved and Declined.
  - Each event has a date block, title, time and place, and when it was
    shared. Then Link, Decline and Approve.
  - A likely duplicate says "Looks like one already listed", and Link
    becomes Compare.
  - The date label is grey: pink stays for act and needs-you.
- **Contact import:**
  - The file card (name, row count, Change), then Source and Columns.
  - "Need a look" comes first: each row names the problem in pink (no
    email, already a contact, company not found) and has one fix (Skip,
    Merge, Fix).
  - Then the ready rows, collapsed after two.
  - The bar says exactly what will happen: "Import 39 contacts".
- **Runs, Sources, Jev and Product health** keep the current admin screens'
  content. They take on Take A's grouped lists and type when they're built,
  without a separate drawing.

## To design, in order

Each batch is drawn in light first, with dark added at the end of the batch.

   - a welcome

   - resume import

   Filters.

   - description sections, with boilerplate collapsed
   - the company card at the end
   - the ⋯ menu: I applied, Not interested, Hide company, Report
4. **Search:** results across jobs, companies and events.
5. **Notifications:** settings per type, the digest time and quiet hours.
6. **Profile:** the resume, the answers bank and links.
7. **Empty and error states:** no matches, an empty Tracker, offline.

### Batch 2: tracking (phase 2)

8. **The application page:**
   - a timeline
   - email-detected steps, each with "Not right? Change"

11. **Stats:**
    - a funnel, the response rate and time to first reply
    - the Sankey, with a shareable image
    - CSV export
12. **Notifications and the daily digest,** as they look on the lock screen.

### Batch 3: auto-apply (phase 3)

### Batch 4: outreach (phase 4)

16. **The company page:** Jobs, People and Events, plus Follow.

    waiting, replied and closed.

21. **Events:** the board, an event page (Register, Add to Calendar, Remind
    me), and submitting an event.
22. **Writing:** the What's new list, the sheet after a big release, and a
    guide.

### Batch 6: pricing (phase 7)

23. **The Plan page,** the paywall at the moment of intent, and a usage meter.

### Batch 7: admin

24. **The in-app admin restyled:** Inbox, Runs, Sources, Jev and Product
    health.
25. **New admin screens:** event review and contact import.

The web, for both mobile and desktop, comes after iOS. It gets its own
